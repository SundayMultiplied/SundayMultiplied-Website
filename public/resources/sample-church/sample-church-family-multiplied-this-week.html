The God Who Made Us Is Near

Sample Church • In the Beginning • Genesis 1:1–2 • This Week

This Week’s Big Idea

God made everything, so we can trust what he says and remember that he is near while he works in our lives.

Scripture

Genesis 1:1–2

1 In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

2 Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.

Scripture quotations are from the Berean Standard Bible.

Remember

What did we hear Sunday?

  • What do you remember about the difference between asking “how?” and asking “who made everything?”
  • What did the pastor say God’s Spirit was doing when the earth was still formless and empty?
  • What is one thing the message helped you understand about God?

Talk About It

  • When friends, videos, or people at school tell us who we should be, how can we decide which voices to trust?
  • Think of something you are still learning—maybe a sport, an instrument, schoolwork, or how to handle frustration. How does it help to know God stays near while we are still growing?
  • When you disagree with someone, what could help you remember that God made that person too?

Look at the Heart

  • Why do we sometimes want our feelings or what other people say to matter more than what God says?
  • When life feels confusing or unfinished, why can it be hard to believe that God is still close?

Family Scenario

A friend tells you, “Everyone gets to decide for themselves what is right. If something makes you happy, nobody should tell you it is wrong.” You want your friend to like you, so it would be easier just to agree.

What would you normally want to do?

Would you agree quickly, argue, stay quiet, or do something else? Why?

What would Jesus want us to remember?

God made us and knows what is good for us. He also made our friend, so we should respond with both honesty and kindness.

What could trusting Jesus look like here?

Take turns saying one or two sentences you could really use. Try to tell the truth without treating your friend like an enemy.

Practice It This Week

Pause and Remember

Once each day, let one family member choose a real decision the family is facing. Pause together and say, “God made us, God knows what is good, and God is near.” Then name one choice that trusts God’s wisdom. Keep it brief—this can happen in the car, at dinner, or when a disagreement begins.

One Question for the Week

What choice right now shows that we trust the God who made us?

Family Prayer

Creator God, you made the whole world, and you made each one of us. You know us better than anyone, and you know what is good. Forgive us when we trust our feelings or other people’s opinions more than we trust you. Thank you for staying near when life feels confusing and while we are still growing. Help us listen to your Word, treat every person you made with dignity, and remember that Jesus brings light into our darkness. Give our family courage to choose what is true and kindness in the way we speak. Amen.

Parent Note

You do not have to teach this perfectly—just start the conversation. You may use every question or only one. The goal is to help your family hear God’s Word, talk about it, and practice it together.

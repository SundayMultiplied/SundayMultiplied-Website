Redefining Greatness

Southside Baptist Church · Week of 2026-08-09

Big Idea

Jesus shows us that true greatness means depending on God, taking sin seriously with His help, and valuing people the way He does.

Scripture

Remember

  • What do you remember about the child Jesus used to teach the disciples about humility and greatness?
  • What did the pastor say Jesus' strong words teach us about how seriously we should take sin?
  • What did the one lost sheep help us understand about people Jesus loves and values?

Talk About It

  1. When do you most want to win, be first, or have somebody notice what you did?
  2. What makes it tempting to excuse something wrong instead of confessing it and asking Jesus for help?
  3. Who is easy to overlook because that person cannot do much for you in return?

Look at the Heart

  1. What do you hope being first, winning, or being noticed will give you?
  2. How can pride make it harder to notice your own sin or care about another person?

Try This Together

Your brother, sister, friend, or teammate gets praised for something you wanted to be noticed for. At the same time, someone else is being left out. You feel like making the first person's success seem smaller and ignoring the person on the outside.

  1. What would you normally want to say or do in that moment?
  2. What would Jesus want us to remember about humility, confessing pride, and valuing people He loves?
  3. What could you say to encourage the person who succeeded, and how could you include the person being overlooked?

Encourage, Confess, Notice

  1. When comparison or pride shows up, encourage rather than compare.
  2. Confess the pride or sin instead of excusing it, and ask Jesus for help.
  3. Notice one person being overlooked and choose a real way to value or include that person.

One Question for the Week

Was I faithful?

Pray Together

Jesus, You are the greatest, and we need Your help. Forgive us when we want to be first, compare ourselves, or have everyone notice us. Teach us to depend on God and be faithful. Help us take sin seriously, confess it instead of excusing it, and trust You and Your Spirit to help us change. Open our eyes to people we overlook. Give us compassion to encourage, include, and value them the way You do. Thank You for loving us and inviting us into God's family when we repent and believe in You. Amen.

Parent Note

You don't have to teach this perfectly. Start the conversation, let your children answer, and stop when the conversation has run its course. You do not need to finish every question. The goal is to hear God's Word, talk about it, and practice it together.
